Privacy Policy

Bookshelf Blast (Android) · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information Bookshelf Blast ("the app") handles. The app is a single-player puzzle game made by an independent developer. There are no user accounts and the developer does not run any servers that receive your data.

Data the developer collects

None. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (high score, number of books collected, unlocked shelves, sound setting and an in-progress game) is saved locally on your device. It is never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Ads are shown between games and, if you choose, as an optional video you can watch to continue a game. You are never required to watch an optional video.

Your choices

  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for the Google Play Families program. The developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Permissions

The app uses internet access to load ads and the vibration permission for haptic feedback. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
